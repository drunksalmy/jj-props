JJ Props — NBA Player Props & Picks
===================================

OPEN IT
  Easiest: go to the website on any phone or computer:
      https://drunksalmy.github.io/jj-props/
  On a phone, open that link and choose "Add to Home Screen" in the browser
  menu. It then opens like a normal app.

  Or on this computer: open the "JJ Props" folder on your Desktop and
  double-click  index.html

  The first time takes about 30–60 seconds while it downloads player stats.
  After that it opens fast (stats are saved in your browser). Keep the tab
  open during games — scores, the lead tracker and live chances refresh
  every 15 seconds on their own.


WHAT'S ON EACH TAB
  Top Picks     Tonight's best plays, and the AUTO PARLAY: choose 5–10 legs and
                a style, and JJ Props fills the slip with the best legs for you.
                Tap any card to see exactly why.
  Games         Who wins, by how many (winning margin vs the sportsbook spread),
                total points, injuries, and the live lead tracker
                (current lead, biggest lead, lead changes, ties, scoring runs).
  Player Props  Every rotation player's points, rebounds, assists, 3-pointers and the
                combos PR, PA and PRA, compared with the real DraftKings line
                (shown as "DK -115 / -105").
                  PR  = points + rebounds      (18 pts + 7 reb = 25 PR)
                  PA  = points + assists       (18 pts + 4 ast = 22 PA)
                  PRA = points + rebounds + assists
                Betting somewhere else? Type that sportsbook's line into the "Line"
                box — the pick and the explanation update instantly (and are remembered).
  How it works  Over/under, spreads, moneylines and parlays explained in plain English.


HOW TO READ A PICK
  OVER 24.5 points ....... he needs 25 or more for the bet to win
  Strong / Good / Lean ... how much we like it ("No clear edge" = skip it)
  The ring (58%) ......... out of 100 bets like this, we expect about 58 wins
  needs 53% .............. how often it must win to be worth betting at that price
                           (the small mark on the ring; the bigger the gap, the better)
  Hit rate L10 7/10 ...... this bet would have won in 7 of his last 10 games
  DK -115 / -105 ......... the DraftKings line and its over / under odds
  Estimate ............... DraftKings has no line for him yet (they usually post
                           player lines on game day), so the app uses his average


THE AUTO PARLAY (on Top Picks)
  1. Pick how many legs: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 or 10.
  2. Pick a style:
       Best value ... legs where our chance beats the DraftKings price the most
       Safest ....... legs most likely to hit
  3. Don't like a leg? Tap the swap button next to it to get the next best one.
  4. "Copy slip" copies the whole slip so you can paste it anywhere.
  Chance all hit, $10 pays, and Expected return show what the slip is worth.
  Remember: every leg has to win. Five legs at 55% each hit only about 1 in 20.


WHY SO FEW PICKS EARLY IN THE SEASON?
  Sportsbook lines are usually close to right. Our chance mixes our model with
  DraftKings' own odds — and in the first weeks, when our numbers are mostly
  from last season (and players have new teams and roles), DraftKings' odds
  count more. So early on you'll mostly see "Slight lean" picks. More picks
  show up as players log games.


LOOK
  The moon / sun button switches between Midnight (dark) and Daylight (light).


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
  "Couldn't reach ESPN's data feed" .... check your internet; the app keeps retrying.
  Numbers look stuck or old ............ gear icon (Settings) → "Reload all data".
  Page is blank ......................... right-click index.html → Open with → Chrome or Edge.


UPDATING THE WEBSITE LATER
  The website comes from your GitHub repository "jj-props":
      https://github.com/drunksalmy/jj-props
  Change a file there (GitHub lets you edit or upload files in the browser) and
  the website updates by itself in about a minute.


WHERE THE DATA COMES FROM
  ESPN's public NBA feed — the same one espn.com uses — for scores, box scores,
  injuries, team records, and DraftKings lines (game lines and player props).
  It isn't an official developer API, so ESPN could change it someday.


IMPORTANT
  Predictions are estimates, not guarantees — even strong picks lose often.
  For people 21+ where sports betting is legal. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
